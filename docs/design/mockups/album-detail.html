Loveless

[ˈlʌvlɪs] • Released: 04 November 1991 • Producers: Kevin Shields, Colm Ó Cíosóig

Recorded across an excruciating two-year migration through nineteen commercial studios in the United Kingdom, Loveless inverted the rock production continuum. Rather than treating distortion and feedback as peripheral decoration, Kevin Shields mobilized continuous microtonal vibrato via the Fender Jazzmaster’s loose tremolo bar held while strumming—a technique christened glide guitar.

Coupled with Yamaha SPX90 reverse-gated reverberation units fed into Vox AC30s and minimal microphone placement, the resulting sound was not synthesized, but an ultra-dense, monophonic acoustic illusion: vocals mixed flat with the guitars, suspended in a permanent vapor state.
