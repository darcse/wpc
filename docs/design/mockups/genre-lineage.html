NODE #SG-01 CANONICAL ANCHOR

UK Shoegaze (First Wave)

1988–1993 // London & Thames Valley, UK

The foundational wall-of-sound movement characterized by detuned glide-guitar strumming with continuous whammy-bar manipulation, 100% wet reverse reverb gates, cascaded gain stages, and buried, androgynous vocal coos acting as additional instruments.
