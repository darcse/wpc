The Glide Architecture

SCHEMATIC v2.4

Deconstructed 4-stage signal cascade responsible for the iconic pitch-wobble and liquefaction.

01
1964 Fender Jazzmaster TRANSDUCER
Action: Ultra-low • Heavy Gauge (.011–.052)

Tremolo arm kept constantly under palm while strumming. Tape applied to collet prevents wobble latency; micro-pitch detune executed physically on every downstroke.

02
Yamaha SPX90 / Alesis Midiverb II PRE-GAIN SHAPE
Early Ref 7 / Reverse 1 • Mix: 100% Wet

Crucial inversion: rack reverse reverb placed before distortion cascades. Guitar tone dissolves into digital acoustic bloom before gain staging.

03
Marshall Shredmaster + ProCo Rat 2 HARMONIC SATURATION
Contour: 8.5 • Gain: Saturated • Hard-Clipped

Crushes the reverse reverb bloom into an unbroken wall of overtones, eliminating transient attacks until picking dynamics become continuous oceanic swell.

04
Vox AC30 + Marshall JCM800 ACOUSTIC DISPLACEMENT
SPL: 124–132 dB • Dual 4x12" Cabs

Tremendous volume forces room-coupling feedback; speakers drive physical air, allowing harmonics to fold back into the Jazzmaster single-coil pickups.
