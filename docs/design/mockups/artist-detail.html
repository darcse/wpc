My Bloody Valentine

/maɪ ˈblʌdi ˈvæləntaɪn/ • Acronym: MBV • Primary Imprints: Creation Records, Island, Domino

Formed in Dublin in 1983 by guitarist Kevin Shields and drummer Colm Ó Cíosóig, My Bloody Valentine fundamentally rewrote the grammar of guitar-based music. Through their seminal 1991 opus Loveless and foundational masterwork Isn't Anything, the group forged a paradigm of oceanic volume, pitch-bent detuning, and whisper-quiet vocal sensuality dubbed "glide guitar."

Shields transformed the instrument from an expressive solo device into a malleable physical mist, continuously operating the tremolo tailpiece while sweeping chords through early-digital rack reverbs set 100% wet. The resulting sound envelope suspended rhythm within shifting harmonic mirages, setting the immutable benchmark for the shoegaze movement.

Loveless

Recorded across nineteen studios with a carousel of bewildered sound engineers. Features "Only Shallow," "To Here Knows When," and "Soon." Kevin Shields achieved the shimmering glide texture by tracking rhythm parts via single microphones pointed into isolated baffle chambers.
